K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

Instance-Based Learner

Non-parametric classification classifying each student based on the majority label of their $K$ nearest academic and skill neighbors using inverse-distance weighting.

Distance Formulation: $d(x, y) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - y_i)^2}$, with weight $w_i = \frac{1}{d(x, y_i)}$

K-Means++ (K++ Clustering)

Unsupervised Seeding

Advanced clustering utilizing probabilistic smart centroid initialization ($D(x)^2$) to segment students into 4 distinct placement archetypes without supervised bias.

Probabilistic Seeding Rule: $P(x) = \frac{D(x)^2}{\sum_{x' \in X} D(x')^2}$, minimizing Lloyd's inertia $\sum_{j=1}^k \sum_{x \in S_j} ||x - \mu_j||^2$
