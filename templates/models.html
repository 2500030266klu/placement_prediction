AdaBoost (Adaptive Boosting)

Sequential Re-weighting

Sequential ensemble learning that builds consecutive decision stumps by adaptively increasing the weights of misclassified student records, compelling subsequent learners to focus on difficult edge-cases.

Sample Weight Update & Estimator Weight: $\alpha_m = \frac{1}{2} \ln \left( \frac{1 - \epsilon_m}{\epsilon_m} \right), \quad w_i^{(m+1)} = w_i^{(m)} \exp(-\alpha_m y_i h_m(x_i))$
n_estimators: 50 base_estimator: DecisionTree(depth=2) learning_rate: 0.8 algorithm: SAMME.R

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

Instance-Based Learner

Non-parametric classification classifying each student based on the majority label of their $K$ nearest academic and skill neighbors using inverse-distance weighting.
