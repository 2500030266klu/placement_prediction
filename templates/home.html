About the System

The Placement Prediction System is an end-to-end machine-learning-powered web application designed to analyze multidimensional student performance metrics and predict campus placement success.

The platform enables students to understand their employability readiness, identify critical skill gaps, and explore expected salary packages, while equipping placement officers with institutional cohort analytics.
